02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

About me.

a few frames along the way.

I'm Idhant — a student, developer, creator, and someone who loves turning ideas into things people can actually use.

I build software, experiment with AI, and enjoy figuring out how things work. My main project, SignBridge, is an AI-powered Indian Sign Language communication system focused on making communication more accessible while keeping user privacy at the center.

Outside of coding, I'm into football, technology, content creation, and constantly learning new things. I like taking on projects that challenge me, whether that's building an app from scratch, experimenting with new technology, or creating something completely random just because I wondered if I could.

I'm still a student, but I'm already building, competing, experimenting, and learning by doing — and I'm just getting started.
